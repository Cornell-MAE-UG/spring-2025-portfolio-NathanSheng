As part of a high school project in 2021, I made a bird feeder that took pictures of birds as they landed to feed. Before I did this project, I felt pretty comfortable with Onshape and 3D printing, but I didn't really know anything about the electrical and CS side of things. This project introduced me to using python and soldering.
