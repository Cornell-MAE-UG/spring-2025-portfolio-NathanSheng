Makes any drone FPV! This was a detachable module that streamed live FPV footage, allowing any drone to be flown FPV style. It had a walksnail FPV camera and antenna, a lipo battery, and a fan for cooling. Without the fan, the camera overheats easily.

The red V1 and V2 camera mounts below were for a high-speed drone that we never really got to work on CUAD. V2 is much better because it is smaller thanks to using a simple linkage instead of gears and a simpler mounting strategy. The grey camera mount in the bottom right doesn't have an adjustable servo-controlled angle but it is also very compact and actually mounts an extra edge-compute spatial camera. However, overheating would defintely have been an issue because of the extra heat and it is completely contained within a transparent cover.
