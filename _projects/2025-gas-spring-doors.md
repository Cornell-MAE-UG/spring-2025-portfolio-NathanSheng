The main purpose of these doors was to hide messy wiring when we went to our first military demo. I'd like to highlight that I designed and built this thing in less than two weeks.These doors use gas springs, Kydex panels, carbon fiber plates and tubes, and ASA 3D printed brackets. The latch mechanism used here is a simple equal force to close - equal force to open tab. I originally wanted to use a clip like ones that are used in plastic covers but decided that quick integration and fit testing of the whole door was more important and than a nicer latch mechanism could be added later.  I also designed the panels and their mounts that you see in the CAD but we never used them.
