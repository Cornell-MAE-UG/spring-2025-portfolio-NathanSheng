I made this drone because despite working on drones for over a year, I'd never actually made a drone on my own from start to finish, so there were parts of the process I knew I was unfamilar with. The idea was that I wanted to make a small toothpick-style drone with prop guards so that I could finally practice making a drone that was completely mine. Since it would have prop guards, you could hopefully easily use it to train line of sight or FPV flying indoors.

If I were to change it I would probably add small landing gear and make the ducts a little taller to cover the props more.
