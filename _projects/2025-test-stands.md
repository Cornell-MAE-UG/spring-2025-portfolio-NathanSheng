The first thing I did for CUAD and for Mavrik, was design test stands. The CUAD had a load cell and polycarbonate shields. The Mavrik one was initially not designed by me, but after the carbon fiber base broke for it during a bend test, I was asked to design a new one and figure out where to put the load cell while I was still at Cornell. I came up with one that used linear rails, but if I were to do it again I would try to simplify it and do it with no moving parts. I was also worried about some binding that could happen with the rails, but the rails should be rated to deal with the expected load. This was also my first time making stainless steel sheet metal parts and using concrete anchors.
